Tank Royale — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-08-29

1. What We Collect

Tank Royale does not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information. The game runs locally in your browser/app. No account is required.

The following data is stored only on your device (localStorage):

  • Game preferences (graphics settings, control layout)
  • Player profile (display name, stats) — never sent to a server

2. Multiplayer

When you join a multiplayer match, a temporary WebRTC peer connection is established. Your IP address may be visible to other players in the same match. No match data is stored on our servers beyond the duration of the session.

3. Analytics

Tank Royale does not use third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or tracking pixels.

4. Children

Tank Royale does not knowingly collect data from children under 13. The game contains cartoon vehicular combat with no blood or gore.

5. Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date above will be updated.
