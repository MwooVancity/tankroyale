Privacy Policy — Tank Royale

Last updated: September 1, 2026

Tank Royale ("we", "us", or "our") operates the Tank Royale mobile game application (the "App"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our App.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following data when you use the App:

  • Google Sign-In: When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and Google Account ID. This is used solely to identify you within the game (display name, leaderboards). We do not store passwords.
  • Device Information: Our advertising partner (Google AdMob) may collect your device advertising ID and app activity data to serve relevant ads. See Section 3.
  • Multiplayer Connection Data: To enable real-time multiplayer, your device establishes peer-to-peer WebRTC connections with other players. IP addresses involved in these connections are transient and not stored by us.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To identify you within the game and display your name to other players
  • To enable multiplayer matchmaking
  • To display advertisements that support free access to the game

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Tank Royale uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and use data including your device's advertising ID and app activity to personalize ads. You can opt out of personalized advertising in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization on Android).

AdMob's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

4. Google Sign-In

We use Google Sign-In for authentication. Google's collection and use of your information is governed by Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

You can revoke Tank Royale's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

5. Data Retention

We retain your display name and Google Account ID for as long as you have an account with us. You may request deletion of your data by contacting us at the email below.

6. Children's Privacy

Tank Royale is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

7. Security

All data transmitted between the App and our servers uses encrypted connections (HTTPS/WSS). We implement reasonable security measures to protect your information.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
