Privacy Policy — Tank Royale

Effective date: August 29, 2026

Tank Royale is a browser-based tank battle game developed by Michael Woo. This policy explains what data the app handles.

Data we do NOT collect

Tank Royale does not collect, store, or transmit:

  • Your name, email address, or any personal identity
  • Your location
  • Your device identifiers
  • Payment information
  • Photos, contacts, or files from your device

Local storage

The app saves game preferences (graphics quality, last-used tank, player nickname) in your device's local storage. This data never leaves your device.

Multiplayer (optional)

If you use multiplayer mode, your chosen player nickname and a randomly generated session ID are sent to a signaling server solely to connect you with other players via WebRTC peer-to-peer. These are discarded when your session ends. No account is required.

Third-party services

The app does not use advertising networks, analytics SDKs, or crash reporting services.

Children

Tank Royale does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children under 13.
